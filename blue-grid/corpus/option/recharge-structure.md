Any site can discharge part of the requirement by recharging rather than
detaining: capturing what falls on the sealed surface and returning it to the
aquifer instead of to the drain. In a city that floods in the monsoon and runs
dry for the rest of the year, one structure answers both problems, which is why
this option needs no particular ground to be available.

How much it can absorb depends on what lies underneath, so it is sized on a
percolation test and not on this screening.
