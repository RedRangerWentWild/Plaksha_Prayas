Where a plot sits on a mapped former bed, the use that discharges the retention
requirement completely is putting the water back. Restoration with public access
on the surrounding land gives the city an asset, and the development value moves
rather than disappearing, through transferable development rights or floor-space
credit exercised on another site.

Karnataka and Maharashtra both carry that mechanism. What is missing is
systematic use of it, which is a question of practice rather than of drafting.
