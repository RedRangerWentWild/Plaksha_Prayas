Where the constraint is a drainage line rather than a bed, development can be
required to leave the channel physically open and build around it. The corridor
stays continuous, the plot keeps most of its area, and the obstruction this
screening flagged does not occur.

Singapore and Rotterdam both require this of sites on a watercourse. It is
harder architecturally than building over the line. The alignment that governs
is the notified one, not the terrain line this tool traced.
