Where full restoration is not available, the site can be built as a designed
retention basin: a sunken park, dry for most of the year, which fills in heavy
rain and releases slowly afterwards. It reads as an amenity and behaves as a
tank, which is why it survives politically where a bare refusal does not.

Chennai and Ahmedabad have both built them. The volume such a basin must hold is
the displacement this screening already computed, so the sizing question is
answered before the design starts.
