The narrower of the two buffer regimes sits barely outside the measurement
uncertainty. Under it, most plots near a lake edge come back as refer-to-survey
rather than reject, because the measurement genuinely cannot separate inside
from outside.

The wider regime is comfortably testable and returns decisive results on the
same measurements. This is a fact about the relationship between the rule and
the available data, not a defect in either. It is also the reason a referral
under the narrow regime should be read as unresolved rather than as a near miss.
