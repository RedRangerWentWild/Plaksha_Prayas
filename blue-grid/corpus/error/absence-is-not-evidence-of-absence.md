A clear result means the satellite record holds no water body, no buffer breach
and no drainage line within the measurement uncertainty of this plot. It is a
statement about the record.

Below the detection floor the record is silent rather than negative. Small
tanks, filled decades before the archive begins, and drains too narrow for the
terrain model all fall into that silence. A clear screening result is a reason
not to prioritise a plot for investigation. It is not an assurance to the
applicant, and it should not be quoted back to one as though it were.
