The budget is dominated by where a shoreline sits inside a source pixel. Every
term this tool adds by its own processing is smaller, and several are zero by
construction.

The practical consequence is that finer encoding, a finer distance transform or
a larger export would buy nothing. The number would gain digits and not
accuracy. Improving on this would take a better source: a surveyed lake boundary
or a fine-resolution elevation model, neither of which is what this tool is
built on.
