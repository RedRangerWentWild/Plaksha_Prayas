Holding the displaced volume on site is what sending nothing extra downhill
means. The requirement is not a new obligation: it is the volume this tool has
already computed for the design storm, read as something to retain rather than
as something to shed.

That keeps it checkable on paper. Plot area, storm depth and the change in
runoff coefficient give the volume, and the volume is the requirement. How much
of it the harvesting rule in force already covers is a comparison the officer
makes, with the figure that binds here.
