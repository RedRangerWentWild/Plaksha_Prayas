A plot's own contribution is its area times the rain that falls on it. What
passes through it is its upstream catchment times the same rain, and on a
channel that can be thousands of times larger.

So for a plot astride a flow path the displaced volume is the wrong headline
number. The relevant quantity is what the obstruction does to water that was
already arriving from uphill, and that depends on the geometry of what is built
and what is left open. This tool reports the catchment size so the scale of that
question is visible; it does not answer it.
