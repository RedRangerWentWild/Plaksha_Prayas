Sealing a surface has two effects and the volume is the smaller one. Water that
soaks in leaves slowly over hours; water that runs off paving arrives in
minutes. The same rainfall therefore reaches the channel as a taller, narrower
pulse.

A drain sized for the old pulse can be overwhelmed by the new one carrying a
similar total. This is why incremental development floods a catchment that no
single development would have flooded, and why the displaced volume from one
plot understates what that plot contributes to the problem.
