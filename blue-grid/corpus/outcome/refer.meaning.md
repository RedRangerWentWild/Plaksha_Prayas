A referral means the measurement cannot answer the question at the resolution
available, and names which question. Either a distance falls within the
uncertainty of its threshold, or the two shoreline records disagree, or a
terrain drainage line passes close enough that the notified alignment has to be
checked.

It is the outcome the tool reaches most often near a lake edge under the
narrower buffer regime, and that frequency is honest rather than evasive. The
action it asks for is specific and cheap: put a surveyor on the plot boundary
against the notified boundary, and the question resolves.
