A rejection at screening means one of three things was found: the footprint
falls inside a water body still present in the record, it falls on a former
water body that held water in most observations, or it falls confidently inside
the configured buffer with the breach larger than the measurement uncertainty.

It is a recommendation to refuse at screening stage, addressed to the
sanctioning authority. It is not itself a refusal, and it does not by itself
establish that a structure already standing there is unlawful. What it does
establish is that the application should not proceed without the notified
boundary being checked on the ground.
