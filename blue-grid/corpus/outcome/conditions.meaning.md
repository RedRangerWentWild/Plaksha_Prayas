A conditions result means no buffer finding was made, but the plot stands very
low relative to the channel it drains into. Ground like that receives water
first and releases it last, whatever is built on it.

The conditions that follow are ordinary ones: on-site retention sized to hold
the runoff the development adds, and a plinth level set above the surrounding
ground. Neither is exotic and both are routinely imposed. The finding is that
they should be imposed here rather than left to the applicant's discretion, and
the height above drainage is the evidence for saying so.
