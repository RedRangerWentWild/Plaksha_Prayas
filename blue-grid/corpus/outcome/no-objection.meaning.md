A clear result means the tool found no recorded water body, no buffer breach and
no drainage line within the measurement uncertainty of the plot.

It is the weakest of the four statements, not the strongest. The other three are
positive findings backed by a measurement; this one is the absence of a finding,
and the record it draws on has a detection floor beneath which it is silent
rather than negative. Read as an instruction, it says this plot does not warrant
prioritising for investigation on satellite evidence. It does not say the plot
may be built on. Only the sanctioning authority says that.
