A referral does not decide the application either way. It identifies a specific
factual question that a survey can settle and that a satellite cannot, and it
asks for that survey.

For an applicant this is a delay with a defined end: establish the plot boundary
against the notified boundary, and the question is answered. For the authority
it is a filter that separates the applications needing a site visit from those
that do not. Treating every referral as an effective refusal, or waving them all
through, both discard the only information the outcome carries.
