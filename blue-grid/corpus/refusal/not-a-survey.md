Every distance here is measured between raster features, not between marked
corners. The plot is an assumed square centred on a click, not a surveyed
boundary, and the water edge is a classification boundary, not a notified one.

A screening result narrows what a surveyor needs to check and tells them where
to start. It does not stand in for the visit. The certificate carries an
unsigned ground-verification block for that reason, and a certificate without
that block completed is a lead rather than a record.
