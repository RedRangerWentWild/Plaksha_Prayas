Costing flood damage needs asset values, occupancy, depth-damage relationships
and a frequency of occurrence. This tool has none of them and cannot produce a
monetary figure for the harm a development causes or the loss a flood inflicts.

Where a cost is what is actually wanted, the volume and the exposure class are
the inputs to hand to someone who holds the rest. Producing a rupee figure from
what is here would require inventing the missing terms, and the number would
carry the authority of the measured ones without their basis.
