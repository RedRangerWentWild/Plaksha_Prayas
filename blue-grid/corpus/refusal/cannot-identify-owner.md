The building layer is machine-derived footprint geometry. It carries no name, no
khata, no survey number and no owner, and nothing in this tool links a footprint
to a person.

A flagged footprint is a location to check against records held elsewhere. Any
attribution of responsibility for it comes from those records and from a site
visit, not from here. Naming or implying a party on the strength of a satellite
flag would be unsupported by the evidence and unfair to whoever was named.
