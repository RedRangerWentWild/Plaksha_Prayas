Whether a particular construction is lawful depends on the notified boundary,
the sanction record, the date of the sanction, the title and the buffer regime
that binds that application. This tool holds none of those, and reads only
satellite and terrain data.

It reports measurements and compares them against a buffer figure it has been
told to apply. That is a screening input for the authority that decides, and for
the applicant it is a reason to check something specific. It is not an opinion
on legality and cannot be cited as one.
