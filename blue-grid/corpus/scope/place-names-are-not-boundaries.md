The study area can be reached by name as well as by clicking it. The names come
from a fixed list of localities, water bodies, roads and channels inside the
mapped rectangle, each held as a single point near its centre.

A centre point is good enough to move the map and not good enough to judge
anything, so searching a locality never produces a screening result. Only a
coordinate does, because only a coordinate says which plot is meant. A name
found here is also not a boundary: it does not establish that any particular
parcel lies within that locality, and it carries no revenue or survey meaning.
