It reconstructs a city's lost water bodies from the satellite record, flags the
buildings standing on them, ranks those by how much catchment they block, and
screens any plot before it is built on.

Three modes: a timeline showing what was there and what happened to it, a plot
check giving a screening decision against a selectable buffer regime, and a
downstream view of the low ground receiving the runoff the lost tanks used to
hold. Everything it reports traces to a published dataset and a stated
threshold.
