The water classification compares 1984 to 1999 against 2000 to 2021. The
imagery on screen is an early composite scrubbed against a recent one, and
neither is the source of the water layers. The land cover used to confirm
construction is from the most recent years only. The last-water-year channel
spans the whole classification archive.

These are four different windows and conflating them produces confident
nonsense. A lake visible on the older imagery is not the same statement as a
pixel in the historic classification. State which window a claim comes from.
