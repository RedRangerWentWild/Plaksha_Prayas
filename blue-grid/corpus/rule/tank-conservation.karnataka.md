Protection of a tank attaches to the tank as recorded land, not to whether it
currently holds water. A tank bed that has dried, silted or been levelled
remains tank land in the record until the record says otherwise.

This is why the historic water edge is measured at all. A plot that is
comfortably clear of every present-day shoreline may still sit on recorded tank
bed, and the satellite record from the earlier epoch is often the only readily
available evidence that it does. The record to check against is the revenue
entry for that survey number.
