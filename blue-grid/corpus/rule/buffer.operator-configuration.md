Karnataka has more than one buffer figure in play. This tool does not decide
which one binds a given application. It applies the ruleset it is told to apply
and records that choice, with its citation, on the screening certificate.

Changing the ruleset changes the outcome, and it is meant to. An officer
selecting the narrower regime and an officer selecting the wider one will get
different screening results from identical measurements, and both results are
correct reports of what was asked.

The measurements themselves do not change with the ruleset. Only the threshold
they are compared against does.
