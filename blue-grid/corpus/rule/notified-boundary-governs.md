The lake boundary in this tool is the edge of a satellite water classification.
The boundary that binds an application is the one in the notified record held by
the revenue department and the lake authority.

Where they agree, the screening result is a fair preview of the survey result.
Where they disagree, the notified boundary wins and the screening result was a
lead that did its job by prompting the check.

Nothing here substitutes for a physical survey of the plot against that
boundary. The certificate carries a sign-off block for exactly that reason.
