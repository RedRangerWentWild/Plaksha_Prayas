The wider buffer was applied prospectively, to sanctions granted after the
order, rather than as an instruction to demolish everything already standing
inside it. A structure predating the order is not automatically unlawful by
virtue of its distance from a lake.

That distinction matters for what this tool's output can be used for. A plot
screened before construction is squarely within scope. An existing structure
flagged by the same measurement is a lead for the authority that holds the
sanction record, not a finding that the building is illegal.
