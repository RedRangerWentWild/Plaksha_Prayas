The flooding that closed the Outer Ring Road and inundated tech parks and
apartment complexes concentrated in the same zones this tool ranks highest for
lost water and blocked flow paths.

That correspondence is evidence that the mechanism is real at catchment scale.
It is not evidence about any particular plot, it is not a prediction that the
same places flood next time, and it must never be offered as one. Citation still
required before this is used in anything that leaves the building.
