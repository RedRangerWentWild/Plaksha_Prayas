The same BBMP survey recorded 204 drain encroachments in Bommanahalli, placing
it fourth among the city's zones.

HSR Layout sits in that zone, and it is the densest cluster of satellite-derived
encroachment this tool finds anywhere in the study area. As with the other
zone-level figures this is corroboration at the scale of a zone. It says nothing
about any individual plot, and must never be repeated as though it did.
