Bengaluru Urban and Rural hold 1547 lakes between them, with 10785 acres of lake
land recorded as encroached.

This study area covers two zones of one city. The figures above are the reason
the method matters more than this particular catchment: a screening check that
runs from public satellite data can be pointed at any of those lakes without new
fieldwork, and the constraint on doing so is attention rather than data.
