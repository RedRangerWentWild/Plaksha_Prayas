The two largest water bodies in this catchment survive as water on the satellite
record while being, in fact, heavily degraded: froth carried onto adjacent
roads, surface fires, and inflow dominated by untreated sewage.

This matters for reading the map honestly. A lake shown as present is a lake
that still holds water in the record. Nothing in this tool measures water
quality, ecological function or whether the water body performs the storage role
its protection assumes. Present is not the same as intact.
