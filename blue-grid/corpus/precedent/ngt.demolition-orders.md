Structures built inside lake and drain buffers in this city have been the
subject of removal orders. The risk a buffer finding represents to an applicant
is therefore not only that sanction is refused, but that a building completed
under a defective sanction is later ordered down.

That is the practical case for screening at approval stage. It is also a general
statement about a line of orders, not advice about any specific plot, and the
specific orders must be cited before this is relied on.
