The survey added 1134 newly recorded drain encroachments to a standing list of
1963 still awaiting removal.

A queue of that length is not cleared in the order complaints arrive, and the
order it is cleared in decides who stays flooded. Ranking structures by how much
upstream catchment each one blocks is a way of ordering that queue by downstream
harm. The ranking is a proposal about priority, not a finding about legality.
