A BBMP survey recorded 523 stormwater drain encroachments in Mahadevapura, more
than in any other zone of the city.

Kasavanahalli, Panathur and Varthur all fall inside that zone, and they are
three of the four places this tool independently ranks highest. The tool has
never seen the survey: it reads satellites and terrain only. That the two line
up was not engineered, which is the only reason the agreement counts for
anything. It describes the zone, not any particular plot inside it.
