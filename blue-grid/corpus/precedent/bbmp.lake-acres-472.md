BBMP identified 472 acres of encroached lake land and has recovered 17.38 acres
of it.

The gap between those two figures is the argument for screening before sanction
rather than enforcement afterwards. Land that is built on is, in practice, land
that stays built on. A plot checked at approval stage costs an officer a few
minutes; the same plot checked after construction costs years and usually ends
in the structure standing.
